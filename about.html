About the Artist

William

Building a photographic archive that feels calm, tactile, and slightly cinematic. The goal is not just to show where I went, but to show how each place changed the pace of seeing.

I like systems with restraint: strong image choices, clean spacing, and interactions that do not compete with the work. The gallery now reflects that better. It is still personal, still a little experimental, but much less interested in showing off code for its own sake.

Base

Beijing, China; Pennsylvania, USA

Cameras

Nikon Z9, Canon R5, Sony A7M IV, Dji Mini 3Pro

Build stack

HTML, CSS, Vanilla JS / Synergy [Gemini & Chatgpt]

Fonts

Manrope, Cormorant Garamond, PingFang SC, Hiragino Sans GB, Noto Sans SC, Georgia, serif, sans-serif

Current focus

Photography, layout systems, and cleaner visual storytelling

Manifesto

Seeking order inside chaos.

I am drawn to places where structure and atmosphere are pulling in opposite directions: temple corridors, mountain weather, city glass, long roads, and artificial light after midnight.

Working rule

I am whatever it takes.

That line stays, but the design around it is now quieter. Better work deserves fewer distractions, fewer forced effects, and more confidence in spacing, sequence, and image choice.
