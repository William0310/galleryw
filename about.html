About the artist

William

I build this gallery as a personal archive for travel photography, field notes, and visual experiments.

The work is drawn toward places where order and atmosphere push against each other: mountain weather, temple corridors, night skylines, old gardens, and long roads. The website is intentionally simple so the photographs can hold the page.

Base

Beijing, China / Pennsylvania, USA

Cameras

Nikon Z9, Canon R5, Sony A7M IV, DJI Mini 3 Pro

Site

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript

Focus

Photography, coding, travel, and driving

Fonts Applied

Manrope, PingFang SC, Hiragino Sans GB, Noto Sans SC, Arial, sans-serif, Cormorant Garamond, Georgia, serif

Manifesto

I am whatever it takes.

Chasing the light that has traveled millions of years, just to kiss the earth for a second.
