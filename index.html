About

Photographs by William.

Wphotographs collects landscapes, temples and cities, mostly from western China and Japan — made on long walks, early mornings and late buses. Each album is laid out to feel a little like the place it came from.

Click any frame to step into its album. Inside, every photo opens full screen, and panoramas can be dragged.
