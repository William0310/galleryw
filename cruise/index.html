Liquid Glass

A sidebar made of glass.

One large sheet of Liquid Glass holds your address bar, tabs and pins. It picks up your wallpaper and the colors behind it, so the browser sits in your desktop instead of on top of it.

  • Large, continuous glass surfaces
  • Spring motion on tabs, panes and buttons
  • Native SwiftUI controls

Adaptive Sidebar

Full screen, fully immersive.

In full screen the sidebar takes on the color of the page you are reading. A sunset, a white homepage, a green hillside: the browser fades into the site and nothing frames it.

Cruistomize

Make any website yours.

Hide the ads, banners and buttons you never use. Change a site's colors and fonts. Cruise remembers your changes for each website.

  • Point at an element and click to hide it
  • Adjust colors and fonts for one site
  • Stays on your Mac. Sign-in and payment fields are never hidden

Pin Tabs

Keep the few that matter.

Pin the sites you open every day. They sit at the top of the sidebar as large tiles, one click away, and stay put while everything else comes and goes.
