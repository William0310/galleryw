What Mate does

Read less. Understand more.

Reads the page

Ask what a long article says, or what a confusing page wants from you.

Translates

Turn a page or a paragraph into a language you read, without leaving it.

Summarizes

Get the short version of a long page, then decide whether to read the rest.

Guides your cursor

Cannot find the right button? Mate moves your cursor to it and shows you where to click.

Private by design

It runs on your Mac. Nothing leaves it.

Mate uses your computer's processor to run open-source models locally. It is fast, it does not send the pages you read to a server, and it is completely free.

  • Open-source models, run on your own processor
  • Your pages and questions stay on your Mac
  • No account, no subscription, free
