Homepage direction sketches

Six ways to escape the normal card grid.

These are intentionally rough layout demos. They show interaction structure and information placement only: cover area, HUD copy, route/progress, and scroll behavior.

Demo A

Contact Sheet

Photographic worktable. Each collection behaves like a large negative on a vertical film roll.

 Demo B

Route Instrument

Sticky route monitor plus large changing collection frames. Strong travel-system feeling.

 Demo C

Typography Takeover

Large place names lead each collection. The image slot follows the title rhythm.

 Demo D

Mapless Atlas

Abstract travel universe. Collections become nodes instead of list items.

 Demo E

Viewfinder

Camera interface metaphor. Scroll cycles through framed collection targets.

 Demo F

Atlas Zoom

Floating nodes expand into full-screen collection detail, then shrink back into the atlas.
