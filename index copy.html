Liquid Glass

The sidebar is the window.

Cruise gives the sidebar one large sheet of Liquid Glass. It picks up your wallpaper and the colors of the page, so the browser feels like part of your desktop instead of a box on top of it.

  • Large, continuous glass surfaces
  • Spring motion on every tab, pane and button
  • Native SwiftUI, so it behaves like the rest of macOS
SwiftUI Follows your wallpaper

Pin Tabs

Keep the few that matter.

Pin the sites you open every day. They sit at the top of the sidebar as large tiles, one click away, and stay put while everything else comes and goes.

Try it. Tap the pin on a tab, or tap a tile to unpin.

Smart Search

One field. Anywhere you want to go.

Type an address, a question or the name of a tab. Cruise works out what you mean and puts the best answer first.

  • Jumps to a site, a search, or a tab you already have open
  • Pinned tabs show up before anything else
  • Press ⌘L from anywhere to start typing
⌘L

    Profiles

    Work, personal, kept apart.

    Each profile has its own tabs, pinned sites, cookies and sign-ins. Switch in one click and nothing crosses over.

    • Separate sign-ins for the same site
    • Its own wallpaper, so you always know where you are
    • Switch without closing a single tab

    Built for the Mac

    Native from the first pixel.

    SwiftUI, all the way

    No web shell around a web page. The interface is made of the same controls as the apps you already use.

    Glass that moves

    Panels stretch, settle and morph with springs. Opening a tab or switching a profile feels like handling an object.

    The page comes first

    Everything lives in the sidebar, so the web page gets the whole window and a clean, rounded frame.
