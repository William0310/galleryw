Modern Adaptation

A research essay on how Journey to the West is connected and adapted to the modern world.

Key Points

Research Summary

  • Cultural Legacy of JTW Journey to the West (JTW) is a famous 16th-century Chinese literary classic with four protagonist, the Monkey King (Sun Wukong), is deeply embedded in Mandarin culture as a symbol of power, bravery, and heroism.
  • Modern Reinterpretations While JTW has seen numerous cultural adaptations including a prominent 1986 TV series and many musical works, cartoons, the award-winning video game Black Myth: Wukong stands out as a very successful interpretation that introduces the character to modern, global generations by video game.
  • The Video Game Strategy and Modification To accommodate people areound the globe and modern, fragmented schedules of most gamers, the creators of Black Myth: Wukong (Game Science) streamlined the narrative by focusing solely on Wukong and his distinct quest to retrieve six relics, rather than putting the original story's complex background and four character team dynamic.
  • The Unparalleled Original Despite the success of different modern alternatives like the video game and the TV show, I believe that no recreation can truly equal the original novel because its sophisticated, standpoint and criticisms of religion, how it protrayed rebellion, and bureaucracy require a deep understand of the overall chinese and mandarin culture.
